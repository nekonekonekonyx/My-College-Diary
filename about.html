About Me 🌸

Hi! My name is Abaloyan Jesse Nicole, and I am a 23-year-old 2nd Year BSIT student at Colegio Sta. Teresa De Avila. I consider myself someone who is naturally curious and adventurous. the kind of person who is eager to explore new ideas, interests, and experiences because that's exactly the reason why I chose to become an IT student.

Outside of academics, I enjoy spending my time reading. My interests range from manga like The Promised Neverland, to light novels such as Omniscient Reader’s Viewpoint, and even classic series like Goosebumps by R.L. Stine. I also explore online platforms like AO3, where I enjoy reading and sometimes writing creative works. In fact, I like writing tragic one-shot stories on a whim.

I also enjoy watching YouTube documentaries about a wide variety of topics, from the origins of balisongs to discussions about mental health systems. Alongside that, I watch anime series, which I personally think is what influences my creativity and imagination.

In terms of personality, I identify as an INFJ, which is one of the 16 personality types from the MBTI (Myers-Briggs Type Indicator). MBTI is a personality framework that categorizes people based on how they think, feel, and interact with the world. As an INFJ, I am often described as introspective, creative, and empathetic, someone who enjoys deep thinking and meaningful ideas.

I chose to pursue Information Technology because I want to understand how modern technology works, especially coding, websites, and social media systems. I want to make sense of the technical side and become familiar with the language and concepts used in this field. My goal is to eventually create my own social application someday, if given the opportunity.

Even though I'm well aware that I'm not the best student in this batch, I'm still having the best time cramming, clutching, passing and discovering new things, because what matters the most for me is that I have the willingness to try and I genuinely enjoy my time learning this course.
